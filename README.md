# Growth Audit — DistroKid

**Live:** https://bterzic123.github.io/distrokid-flow-rebuild/

Five ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic DistroKid already has, plus a clickable prototype of the iOS sign-up flow and Choose Your Plan, rebuilt in DistroKid's own design. Only the order of the screens changed.

Prepared by Adapty, November 2026.

## The five ideas

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Move sign-up behind the paywall | CR +8% · ARPU +17% |
| 02 | Ask for notifications after the purchase | CR +10–15% |
| 03 | Open the plans on their release and your 4.9 | CR +15–20% |
| 04 | Let three taps pick the plan | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Offer Musician when they leave Musician+ | ARPU +10–15% |

Run together: up to +25% conversion to purchase and +30% ARPU.

## Grounding

Every price, rating, plan line and claim comes from DistroKid's own App Store listing, plans page and in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for DistroKid.
